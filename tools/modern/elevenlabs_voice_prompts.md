# ElevenLabs Voice Design prompts — Modern juke joint cast

Where: Voices → My Voices → Add a new voice → Voice Design. Paste the **Prompt** and replace the auto "Text to preview" with the **Preview** text. Generate (3 options), pick one, save it with the name in the heading.

## How to get the best results (from ElevenLabs' Voice Design guide)
- The prompts follow ElevenLabs' recommended order: language and dialect → gender, age → quality → persona → emotion → timbre, pacing, delivery.
- Write your own preview text that matches the character. A longer preview gives a steadier, more expressive voice, so keep it to 100 characters or more.
- **Guidance Scale:** raise it if the dialect or age drifts; lower it if the audio sounds rough or strained.
- Leave out FX words like "echo", "reverb", "radio", "phone" or "tape", because they lower quality. The game already adds room tone.
- Use "accent" or "dialect" only for regional speech. For delivery, describe intonation instead.
- Change one phrase at a time between tries, for example "thick" against "slight" Southern drawl.
- When you voice the lines, use Eleven v4 (ElevenLabs now recommends it over v3) with audio tags, e.g. `[excited] Jackpot!`, `[shouting] Gate crasher!`, `[laughs] Look at you go!`.

## bartender — Velma
Prompt:
Native English, African American Southern dialect from the 1940s Mississippi Delta. Female, mid-40s. Studio quality. Persona: sassy, big-hearted juke joint bartender. Emotion: warm, teasing, confident. A rich, smoky alto with a slightly husky edge and a slow, honeyed Southern drawl. She projects easily across a noisy, crowded room, leaning into endearments like "sugar" and "honey" with a knowing smile in her voice. Natural and grounded, never cartoonish.

Preview:
Come on up to the bar, sugar. Bar's open, honey, step on up. Here's your nickel, now go play us something good. Lord have mercy, a special! Drinks on the house!
